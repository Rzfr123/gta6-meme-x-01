A GTA 6 launch-day meme: a gamer slumped at a loading bar stuck at 99%
after a 13-year wait, while the make.fun ghost looks on — his AI agent
already won real money on make.fun.

Entry: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01

`meme.png` (1024×1024) is built by `render.py` (Python + PIL) from an
original AI-generated Vice City / Leonida scene in `assets/` and the real
make.fun ghost from the press kit (`assets/mark-1024.png`, also
`mark.svg` / `wordmark.svg` from https://make.fun/press/). No copyrighted
GTA art is used.

`main.ts` copies the committed `POST.json` to the output dir and nothing
